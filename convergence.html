#6
PfCDPK1 — Phenothiazine Surprise
14 kinase inhibitors + trifluoperazine (antipsychotic phenothiazine) · Priority #5 & #6
AEE788 EGFR/VEGFR — Priority #5 AT7867 Akt inhibitor dacomitinib EGFR approved mitoxantrone Vina −7.8 bafetinib Vina −10.43 ruboxistaurin Vina −11.72 ★ highest trifluoperazine phenothiazine — non-kinase ★
Mechanistic Rationale
CDPK1 has calmodulin-like regulatory domains. Phenothiazines bind calmodulin with well-established affinity → mechanistically coherent, non-ATP-site engagement
Caution
CNS side effects; built to cross BBB — excellent mechanistic probe but not a practical oral antimalarial candidate
#7
PfDHFR-TS — 3 Chemotypes, 2 Pockets
Resistance-insulated scaffold-hopping · Priority Hypothesis #7
Pocket 1 — Catalytic
pyrimethamine + WR99210 + iclaprim — classical antifolates at the active site
Pocket 2 — Second Site
pentamidine (aromatic diamidine) — insulated from classical resistance mutations. Zero pocket overlap with pyrimethamine.
⚠️ Second-site pose needs ground-truth structural validation (X-ray or cryo-EM) before claiming mechanism.
#8
PfA-M1 Aminopeptidase — HDAC/Zn-Chelator Convergence
M-344 + vorinostat + puromycin · Priority Hypothesis #8
Mechanism
Hydroxamate zinc coordination is plausible on Zn-metalloaminopeptidase. Published M1 inhibitor programs confirm zinc-chelation approach.
Caution
Puromycin is a singleton; broad HDAC selectivity concerns for M-344 and vorinostat. Each compound needs individual validation.
